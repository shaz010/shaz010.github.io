Privacy Policy

Ascend · Last updated September 30, 2026

Overview

Ascend is a language learning app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

Ascend does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All app data (including your streak count) is stored locally on your device only.

Microphone & Speech

Ascend uses your device's text-to-speech system to read conversation lines aloud. This is processed entirely on-device. We do not record, transmit, or store any audio.

Analytics

Ascend does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

Children

Ascend does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no user accounts and no data collection of any kind.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
